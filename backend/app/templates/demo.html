Reading rules: the source files contain detections only — a missing row is not evidence of no fire (it may be no observation, an invalid value or a cloud-obstructed pass). MODIS (~1 km) and VIIRS (375 m) detections are not interchangeable and are never merged. FRP is instantaneous power in MW and is never summed into energy. Agreement between sensors is cross-sensor consistency, not accuracy.
